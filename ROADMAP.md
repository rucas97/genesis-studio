# Roadmap

## Phase 1 — The seed (months 1–3)
One protein. One mutation. Real-time stability scoring. A minimal pipeline. A working bridge.

## Phase 2 — The foundation (months 4–9)
Protein library. Real node canvas. Batch execution. Reproducibility. AI co-scientist in first useful form.

## Phase 3 — First emergence (months 10–18)
Constrained Emergence Engine. One tissue. One disease model. Short biological timescales.

## Phase 4 — The platform (months 19–36)
Multi-tissue, multi-disease, longer timescales, in silico intervention testing.

## Beyond
Multi-organ, population-level, AI co-scientist generating hypotheses no human has considered.
